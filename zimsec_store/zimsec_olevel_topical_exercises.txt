SECTION 4: CIRCLE GEOMETRY & PROPERTIES
================================================================================
Sample ZIMSEC Theorems & Questions:
Key Theorems Tested:
1. Angle at center is twice angle at circumference subtended by same arc (AOC = 2 * ABC).
2. Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal.
3. Angle in a semi-circle is a right angle (90°).
4. Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral sum to 180° (A + C = 180°).
5. Tangent to a circle is perpendicular to radius at point of contact (90°).
6. Alternate segment theorem: Angle between tangent and chord equals angle in alternate segment.
7. Tangents from an external point to a circle are equal in length.

Sample ZIMSEC Questions:
- ABCD is cyclic quadrilateral with center O. Given angle ADB = 28°, CBD = 47°, calculate BAD, CDP, CAB, BCD. [4004/1 J2020 Q18]
- TA and TB are tangents to circle center O at A and B. TDO is straight line, ATO = 36°. Calculate AOD, AOB. [4008/1R N2014 Q3]

SECTION 5: CONSTRUCTIONS & LOCI
================================================================================
Rules & Standard Loci:
1. Locus of points at a given distance from a point P -> Circle centered at P.
2. Locus of points equidistant from two points A and B -> Perpendicular bisector of segment AB.
3. Locus of points equidistant from two intersecting lines AB and BC -> Angle bisector of ABC.
4. Locus of points at a given distance from a straight line AB -> Parallel line pair at distance d.

SECTION 7: CO-ORDINATE GEOMETRY & FUNCTIONAL GRAPHS
================================================================================
Formulas & Topics:
- Slope/Gradient m = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1)
- Line equation y = mx + c or ax + by = c
- Parallel lines have equal gradients (m1 = m2). Perpendicular lines satisfy m1 * m2 = -1.
- Distance formula d = sqrt((x2 - x1)^2 + (y2 - y1)^2)
- Turning points of quadratic curves y = ax^2 + bx + c at x = -b / (2a).

SECTION 11: MEASURES, MENSURATION & KINEMATICS
================================================================================
- Mensuration Formulas:
  * Circle: Area = pi*r^2, Circumference = 2*pi*r
  * Sector: Arc length = (theta/360)*2*pi*r, Area = (theta/360)*pi*r^2
  * Cylinder: Volume = pi*r^2*h, Curved Surface Area = 2*pi*r*h, Total Surface Area = 2*pi*r*h + 2*pi*r^2
  * Cone: Volume = (1/3)*pi*r^2*h, Curved Surface Area = pi*r*l (l = slant height = sqrt(r^2 + h^2))
  * Sphere: Volume = (4/3)*pi*r^3, Surface Area = 4*pi*r^2
  * Trapezium: Area = 0.5 * (a + b) * h
  * Prism: Volume = Base Area * Height
- Speed-Time & Velocity-Time Kinematics Graphs:
  * Acceleration = Slope/Gradient of v-t graph
  * Distance Travelled = Area under v-t graph (triangles, trapeziums, rectangles)
  * Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time

================================================================================
- Probability P(E) = Number of favorable outcomes / Total outcomes

- Mean = sum(f*x) / sum(f); Median = middle value; Mode = highest frequency.

SECTION 13: TRIGONOMETRY, BEARINGS & VECTORS
================================================================================
- Right-angled trigonometry: SOH CAH TOA (sin theta = opp/hyp, cos theta = adj/hyp, tan theta = opp/adj)
- Non-right-angled trigonometry:
  * Sine Rule: a / sin A = b / sin B = c / sin C
  * Cosine Rule: a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc * cos A
  * Triangle Area = 0.5 * a * b * sin C
- Bearings: 3-figure angles measured clockwise from True North (000° to 360°). Reverse bearing = (theta + 180°) mod 360°.
- Vector Geometry: Position vectors OP, column vectors (x, y)^T, vector magnitude |v| = sqrt(x^2 + y^2), ratio divisions and parallel vectors (u = k*v).
