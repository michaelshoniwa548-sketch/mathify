ZIMSEC MATHEMATICS SYLLABUS & EXAM SPECIFICATIONS (ZIMBABWE SCHOOL EXAMINATIONS COUNCIL)

1. ORDINARY LEVEL MATHEMATICS (Syllabus 4075 / 4008 / 4028)
- Structure:
  * Paper 1 (50%): Non-calculator paper. Short answer questions covering all syllabus topics.
  * Paper 2 (50%): Structured long questions. Section A (Compulsory), Section B (Choice of 4 out of 6 questions).

- Core Syllabus Topics:
  1. Number & Operations:
     - Sets and Venn Diagrams (Union, Intersection, Complement, Cardinality)
     - Fractions, Decimals, Percentages, Ratios, Standard Form
     - Indices and Logarithms (Base 10)
     - Consumer Arithmetic: Simple & Compound Interest, Depreciation, Tax, Profit/Loss
  2. Algebra & Graphs:
     - Algebraic Expressions, Factorization, Expansion
     - Solving Linear, Quadratic, and Simultaneous Equations
     - Quadratic Formula: x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / (2a)
     - Linear Inequalities and Regions on Graphs
     - Function Notation f(x), Composite Functions, Inverse Functions f⁻¹(x)
     - Linear, Quadratic, and Cubic Function Graphs
  3. Geometry & Trigonometry:
     - Angle Properties of Triangles, Polygons, Parallel Lines, Circle Theorems
     - Pythagoras Theorem and Right-Angled Trigonometry (SOH CAH TOA)
     - Sine Rule: a/sin(A) = b/sin(B) = c/sin(C)
     - Cosine Rule: a² = b² + c² - 2bc cos(A)
     - Area of Triangle: ½ ab sin(C)
     - Bearings (3-digit true bearings measured clockwise from North)
  4. Mensuration:
     - Perimeter, Area of 2D shapes (Trapezium, Sector, Segment)
     - Surface Area and Volume of Prisms, Cylinders, Pyramids, Cones, Spheres
  5. Matrices & Transformations:
     - 2x2 Matrix Multiplication, Determinant, Inverse Matrix
     - Transformations: Translation, Reflection, Rotation, Enlargement (Center & Scale Factor)
     - Matrix representation of transformations
  6. Vectors & Bearings:
     - Position Vectors, Column Vectors, Vector Geometry proofs
  7. Statistics & Probability:
     - Mean, Median, Mode, Grouped Frequency Tables
     - Cumulative Frequency Curves (Ogives), Interquartile Range
     - Probability of Single, Mutually Exclusive, and Independent Events (Tree Diagrams)

2. ADVANCED LEVEL MATHEMATICS (Syllabus 6042 / 9164)
- Modules:
  * Pure Mathematics 1 & 2: Polynomials, Binomial Theorem, Trigonometric Identities, Calculus (Differentiation & Integration), Vectors in 3D, Complex Numbers, Differential Equations.
  * Mechanics (Paper 3/4): Kinematics, Forces and Equilibrium, Newton's Laws, Projectiles, Friction.
  * Statistics (Paper 5/6): Discrete and Continuous Random Variables, Normal Distribution, Hypothesis Testing, Correlation & Regression.

3. ZIMSEC PEDAGOGICAL RULES FOR TUTORING:
- Always show step-by-step working as required by ZIMSEC marking schemes (M1 for Method, A1 for Accuracy, B1 for Independent mark).
- Remind students when Paper 1 rules apply (no calculator allowed).
- Use metric units (m, cm, km, kg) and Zimbabwean financial context (ZWG / USD) where applicable.
